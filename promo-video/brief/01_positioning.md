## 0. What the research says (compressed)

| Platform | How it positions itself | What users / creators actually complain about |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | "Capture and share the world's moments" → now a Reels/recommendation engine | Algorithm fatigue: 55% of Gen Z creators have considered quitting in 2026; reach collapsing for small/mid creators after the late‑2025 shift to profile‑level ranking; likes devalued; feed is ads + recommended strangers + AI slop. Mosseri spends as much time fielding complaints as launching features. |
| **RedNote / Xiaohongshu** | Lifestyle + social commerce; "zhongcao" (planting desire) through authentic notes | Creators do unpaid marketing labour ("Key Opinion Consumers" are rarely paid); small creators struggle for visibility and brand deals; 10%+10% Dandelion fee; non‑Chinese creators can't fully monetize (ID / bank requirements). Feed is beautiful but increasingly commercial. |
| **OnlyFans** | Creator subscription / "fan" monetization | Flat 20% cut; **zero discovery** — no feed, no browse, no recommendations, 100% of traffic must be brought from other platforms; median creator makes ~$131/mo; creators migrating to Fansly/Fanvue for discovery + tools. |
| **Pinterest** | "Visual discovery", inspiration | Became the poster child for AI slop (AI ≈57% of online material); artists say hand‑made work gets mislabelled as AI; had to ship "dial down AI" toggles. |
| **Cosmos** | "A home for your mind"; no algorithm selling you things, no like counts, AI used to *research* images (credit the maker), never to generate; de‑ranks low quality | Loved by designers; tiny social layer, not a creator income platform. |
| **Glass** | Photography community; no public likes / follower counts; paid membership = no ads | Niche, photographers only, no creator earnings. |
| **Cara** | Artist‑first, anti‑AI‑scraping (Glaze built in), no VC | Artists tired of "move to a new app and start from zero". |
| **Are.na** | Slow, intentional, "knowledge garden" | Deliberately un‑mass‑market. |
| **Substack / Patreon** | "Built for writers to turn work into a subscription business" / "creator‑direct, no algorithm or advertiser in between" | Great monetization, poor discovery, no visual feed; Patreon now adding "Quips" to compete with social. |
| **Bluesky / Threads / Lemon8** | High‑signal community, ad‑free (Bluesky); photo showcase (Threads); "RedNote for the West" (Lemon8) | Bluesky: better photo quality in 2026 but unclear revenue, no creator tools; Lemon8: ByteDance feed, same algorithm anxieties. |

**The gap we own:** nobody combines *all three* — (1) a feed that is genuinely beautiful and human‑curated, (2) real creator tooling, (3) real discovery **and** real income. Each incumbent has one, at most two. The premium "quality" apps (Cosmos, Glass, Are.na) have taste but no money; the money apps (OnlyFans, Patreon) have no taste and no discovery; the scale apps (IG, RedNote) have reach but a feed the user no longer trusts.

**Language the category is converging on** (we should *not* parrot it literally): "no algorithm", "no likes", "AI‑free", "creator‑first", "authentic", "community not content". These are now table stakes / clichés. We say the same ideas through *evidence* on screen, not slogans.

## 1. Positioning statement

**LUNE is the first social platform where every piece of work is chosen, not ranked.** It is a hand‑curated home for photographers, filmmakers, designers, writers and lifestyle creators who are tired of making more to be seen less: a feed with no filler, no ads, no synthetic noise — only finished, beautiful work — paired with a studio of creator tools (capture, edit, present, publish, price, sell) and a direct line from audience to income with a transparent split. For the audience it feels like a gallery you'd pay to walk through; for creators it feels like finally being shown to the right people, and paid for it. Where Instagram optimizes for time‑on‑app, RedNote for commerce, and OnlyFans for transactions, LUNE optimizes for *the quality of what gets seen* — and lets the money follow the work.

One‑liner for internal use: *Curated like a gallery. Built like a studio. Pays like a patron.*

## 2. Value props, ranked by promo impact

Ranked by (a) how sharply it contrasts with what the audience already feels about IG / RedNote / OF, (b) how *visual* it is in a 40‑second film.

| # | Value prop | On‑screen line (EN, ≤6 words) | Why it ranks here / how it reads in film |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Curated, not ranked.** Every post on the feed is selected by humans (editors + community curators), not pushed by an engagement algorithm. | **Chosen, not ranked.** | The single sharpest contrast with every incumbent. | 2 | **Zero junk.** No low‑quality video, no AI slop, no ads, no reposts. The feed has a floor. | **No filler. No noise.** | Direct hit on the 2026 "slop" fatigue (Pinterest, IG). Easy to dramatize: chaos → silence → one image. |
| 3 | **A real studio of creator tools.** Capture, edit, color, type, layout, portfolio pages, presets, scheduling — inside the app, built to the same aesthetic bar. | **Make it here.** (alt: *Tools worthy of the work*) | The product proof point; differentiates from Cosmos/Glass/Cara who only *host*. | 4 | **Get paid directly, transparently.** Subscriptions, tips, drops, commissions, licensing; a published creator split, no external‑traffic dependency. | **Your work. Your terms.** (alt: *Paid for the work*) | OnlyFans/Patreon strength with none of the stigma; keep it elegant, no dollar signs. One quiet UI beat: "Supporter joined". |
| 5 | **Be discovered by taste, not by luck.** Human curators + taste‑based recommendation surface new creators to the right people from day one. | **Seen by the right people.** | Answers the #1 small‑creator pain (reach collapse, OF's zero discovery). | 6 | **A calm place to look.** No like counts on the wall, no autoplay avalanche, slow scroll, full‑bleed. | **Look. Slowly.** | Audience‑side emotional payoff; use as tone, not as its own beat. |

| 2 | **Only the work worth seeing.** ★ | Top pick. Audience‑side promise; "worth" implies curation and value (money) at once. |
| 3 | Where good work lives. | Warm, slightly generic. |
| 4 | Made to be seen. | Creator‑side; plays on "made" (tools) + "seen" (discovery). Risk: close to several camera ads. |
| 5 | A feed with a floor. | Insider‑clever, explains the quality bar; may need setup. |
| 6 | Less feed. More work. | Punchy, slightly negative; good for a cutdown. |
| 7 | The quiet gallery. | Mood line; too passive as a master tagline. |
| 8 | Taste is the algorithm. | Smart but uses the word "algorithm" (we'd rather not centre the enemy). |

**Why ★1 and ★2:** #1 is the sharpest single idea we own and is defensible (no incumbent can say it). #2 is the emotional promise the audience feels and also flatters creators ("your work is worth seeing"). Use #1 as master; #2 as the opening/closing line of the film and as the App Store subtitle. Both survive a CN swap in ≤6 characters.

## 5. Audience and tone

### Who the film must convince — creators first, audience as witness
- **Primary (convince):** serious creators — photographers, filmmakers, illustrators, designers, writers, stylists, chefs, architects — age ~22–40, already on IG/RedNote, with 2k–200k followers, whose reach has fallen and who feel their work is drowned. They bring the content; without them there is no feed. The film must make *them* feel "this place is built for work like mine, and it will pay".
- **Secondary (seduce):** the taste‑driven audience — people who save more than they like, who follow magazines and galleries, who are exhausted by slop. They are shown the *result* of the creators' presence: a feed you want to live in. If creators come, this audience follows; the film can address them with the opening/closing line.
- **Tertiary (reassure, implicitly):** press and investors — the film should look like it already has taste, i.e. the film *is* the product proof.

### Feelings to evoke (in order through the film)
1. **Recognition of fatigue** (2–4 s max): the noise you already know — fast, loud, cluttered. Don't dwell; don't name competitors.
2. **Relief / exhale**: a hard cut to quiet; black, then one image; slower cadence; a breath.
3. **Desire**: full‑bleed, gorgeous, varied work (photo, film, type, objects, food, architecture) — the feed as a gallery. The viewer should want to *look longer*.
4. **Capability**: hands making things; tools that feel like real instruments; the UI shown as precise and calm. Pride, not hype.
5. **Dignity**: being paid shown as a small, quiet beat — a name joining, a number updating — never flashy.
6. **Belonging / invitation**: the name, the line, one call to action.

### Tone words
Editorial. Gallery‑quiet. Tactile. Confident, not boastful. Cinematic, slow cuts, generous negative space, serif + grotesk type pairing, real textures (paper, film grain, hands, light), muted palette with one warm accent. Think Aesop / Kinfolk / Apple "Shot on iPhone" restraint, not startup launch sizzle.

### What to avoid
- Cliché copy: "Join the revolution", "Connect with friends", "Share your moments", "Unleash your creativity", "Where creators thrive", "Built for the next generation", "Welcome to the future of social", "Be authentic", "Express yourself".
- Centring the enemy: no literal Instagram/RedNote/OnlyFans UI parodies, no crossed‑out logos, no "no algorithm" banner (use *Chosen, not ranked* instead — say what we do).
- Sexual or "fan‑site" coding: the OnlyFans adjacency must be read as *patronage*, not adult content. No lingerie, no "unlock", no DMs with hearts, no dollar‑sign rain.
- Hype grammar: fast whip‑pans, glitch transitions, EDM drops, neon gradients, emoji, confetti, "🚀".
- Generic stock: smiling diverse group looking at a phone and laughing; drone over a city; typing‑on‑laptop.
- Metrics theatre: follower counts, like counters ticking up, "10x your reach".
- Over‑explaining features: no more than 3 tool beats; one line each; the film is a feeling, not a demo.
- Lines longer than 5–6 words — every on‑screen line must survive translation to ≤8 Chinese characters.

### Call to action (end card)
`LUNE` wordmark · *Chosen, not ranked.* · "Now accepting creators." (alt: "Request an invite.") — invitation‑only wording reinforces curation; keep the CTA swappable.
